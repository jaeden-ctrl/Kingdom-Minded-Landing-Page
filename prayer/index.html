01
The Invitation

Why we're opening the door.

Our team has been meeting on Monday mornings to pray together. It became the half hour that set the tone for everything else. Markets rise and fall, but God's promises don't, and no believer should have to walk through any season alone. So we decided to open the invitation to our whole industry.

A word from Tyson and Jaeden

Video coming soon

02
If This Is You

You're a son or daughter before you're anything else.

Monday marks a new week. When we meet, we pray and declare into that week with intentionality.

You've walked through seasons where the deals didn't close the way you planned, and you kept showing up anyway.

You believe God put you in this business on purpose, and your work is part of what He's doing.

You know God is with you. Some mornings you need a group of people to stand in agreement with you and declare His promises.

The Season We're In

A way in the wilderness.

“Behold, I am doing a new thing; now it springs forth, do you not perceive it? I will make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert.”

Isaiah 43:19 ESV

Israel ate manna in the desert for forty years, and it never missed a morning. God was faithful in the wilderness, and He's faithful in this market. On Mondays we pray for this week's bread with our eyes on the promise.

03
What We Pray For

Bring all of it. He can carry it.

No request is too big, and none of them are too small. These are the things people bring to the call most weeks.

Your business

Your pipeline, your partners, the files stuck in underwriting, and favor with the people you serve.

The market

We pray for a shift in rates and inventory, and we trust the One who holds both.

Provision

Provision for this month, wisdom with what you have, increase for what's ahead, and freedom from what you owe.

Your household

Your marriage, your kids, your parents. The people you're building all of this for.

Your mind

Peace over anxiety, rest instead of the 2 AM math, and a mind set on what God says instead of what the headlines say.

Your body

Healing, strength, and energy for the work in front of you. Health for you and the people you love.

Your spirit

A fresh hunger for God and His Word, and bold faith for whatever this week brings.

Each other

The families we serve, the competitors down the street, and the industry we all share.

04
How It Goes

Thirty minutes that set the week.

Keep your camera on or off. Pray out loud or just listen. Come every week or whenever you can. You're welcome either way.

  1. 01

    We open in prayer

    A quick welcome, and then we hand the week to God before it hands us anything.

  2. 02

    We open the Word

    A story from Scripture and what it says about who you are and the season you're in.

  3. 03

    We declare it out loud

    We speak God's promises over our businesses, our homes, our health, our finances, our governments.

  4. 04

    We pray for each other

    Drop a request in the chat or speak it out. Someone will pray for you by name.

  5. 05

    Back to work, covered

    You head into your week covered in prayer and ready for what God has for you.

05
From the Word

The stories we keep coming back to.

God has always worked through people in business. Joseph ran an economy. Lydia sold purple cloth. Paul made tents. Scripture has a lot to say to people who work for a living, and it says it to us on Monday mornings.

Genesis 37–50

Joseph

Thirteen years passed between the dream and the palace, through a pit and a prison. God wasted none of it, and in the end Joseph fed the nations through a famine.

Exodus 16

The wilderness

Manna came every morning, enough for that day. You couldn't store it up, so you trusted Him again the next day, and He never let them down.

Nehemiah 4

Nehemiah

They rebuilt the wall in fifty-two days, with a tool in one hand and a weapon in the other. Build boldly, and guard what God has given you.

2 Kings 4

The widow's oil

The oil kept pouring as long as there were empty jars to fill. She had to go borrow the jars first, and God met her there.
