Kingdom Minded · Symbol Exploration

Five symbols, no letters.

Each of these comes from the call itself or from Scripture already on the page, not from the usual crowns and crosses. Each is shown large, on white, in the top menu and at browser-tab size.

A

Cornerstone

Stones built up from one solid gold block: the cornerstone that everything else rests on. "Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone" (Ephesians 2:20). It also speaks straight to people whose work is building, buying and financing homes. It doesn't look like any other faith brand, though it's less clear at tab size.

Covering

A house with a small cross above the roof's peak. It says "covered in prayer" and "homes," the two things everyone on the call has in common. It's the most literal of the five.

New Week

A sun coming up over the horizon with seven rays, one for each day of the week. Monday is the first light of the week, and the call is where you start it. It's warm and hopeful.

Rivers in the Desert

Dunes with water running through them, from the verse already on the page: "I will make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert" (Isaiah 43:19). It speaks to a hard market directly. It's the most artistic of the five and the softest at tab size.
